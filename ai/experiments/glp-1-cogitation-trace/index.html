Can you find GLP-1 like molecule in dog or cow genome sequence?

An open-weight model, given this one question and a computer, spent 22 minutes doing genomics. It pulled records from NCBI, re-derived the gene from raw DNA, checked splice sites and copy number, and wrote a sourced report. This page shows every step of that run: what the model thought, the scripts it wrote, what the computer answered, and where it went wrong.

Review notes

These annotations were added after the run by a reviewer reading the trace; they are not part of the model's output. Failures are steps that errored. Silent errors are wrong results that ran without an error, including mistakes that reached the final answer. Sound moves are the steps that made the research credible.
